SecureBank Customer Service Handbook
====================================

Chapter 1: Account Management
------------------------------

1.1 Password Reset Procedure
To reset your password, follow these steps:
1. Visit www.securebank.com/reset
2. Enter your account number or registered email address
3. You will receive a verification code via SMS or email
4. Enter the verification code on the website
5. Create a new password (minimum 8 characters, must include uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character)
6. Confirm your new password
7. You will receive a confirmation email

If you are unable to reset your password online, please call our support line at 1-800-BANK-HELP (1-800-226-5435) or visit your nearest branch with a valid photo ID.

1.2 Account Balance Inquiry
You can check your account balance through the following channels:
- Online Banking: Log in at www.securebank.com
- Mobile App: Download SecureBank Mobile from App Store or Google Play
- ATM: Insert your debit card at any SecureBank ATM
- Phone: Call 1-800-BANK-HELP and follow the automated prompts
- Branch: Visit any SecureBank branch with valid identification

1.3 Account Types
SecureBank offers the following account types:
- Basic Checking: No minimum balance, $5/month fee (waived with direct deposit)
- Premium Checking: $1,500 minimum balance, no monthly fees, earns 0.05% APY
- Savings Account: $100 minimum to open, earns 0.50% APY, 6 withdrawals/month limit
- Money Market: $2,500 minimum, earns 1.25% APY, limited check-writing privileges
- Certificate of Deposit (CD): Terms from 3 months to 5 years, rates from 2.00% to 4.50% APY

Chapter 2: Card Services
-------------------------

2.1 Lost or Stolen Card
If your debit or credit card is lost or stolen:
1. Immediately call our 24/7 hotline: 1-800-CARD-STOP (1-800-227-3786)
2. Log into online banking and navigate to Card Services > Block Card
3. A temporary card can be issued at any branch within 30 minutes
4. A permanent replacement card will be mailed within 5-7 business days
5. Review your recent transactions for any unauthorized activity

2.2 Travel Notifications
Before traveling, please notify us to prevent your card from being blocked:
- Online Banking: Card Services > Travel Notification
- Mobile App: More > Travel Notification
- Phone: Call 1-800-BANK-HELP
Please provide: destination country, travel dates, and contact number abroad.

2.3 Transaction Disputes
If you notice an unauthorized transaction:
1. Contact us within 60 days of the statement date
2. File a dispute through online banking or by calling 1-800-BANK-HELP
3. Provide transaction details: date, amount, merchant name
4. A provisional credit may be issued within 10 business days
5. Investigation typically completes within 45-90 days

Chapter 3: Loan Services
--------------------------

3.1 Personal Loans
SecureBank offers personal loans with the following features:
- Loan amounts: $1,000 to $50,000
- Terms: 12 to 60 months
- Fixed APR: 5.99% to 17.99% (based on creditworthiness)
- No origination fees
- No prepayment penalties

Required documents:
- Valid government-issued photo ID
- Proof of income (last 2 pay stubs or tax returns)
- Proof of address (utility bill or lease agreement)
- Social Security Number for credit check

3.2 Mortgage Information
- Fixed-rate mortgages: 15-year and 30-year terms available
- Adjustable-rate mortgages (ARM): 5/1 and 7/1 options
- Down payment: Minimum 3% for first-time homebuyers, 10% standard
- Pre-approval process takes 1-3 business days
- Closing typically takes 30-45 days after approval

Chapter 4: Digital Banking
---------------------------

4.1 Mobile Banking Features
The SecureBank Mobile App allows you to:
- Check balances and transaction history
- Transfer funds between accounts
- Pay bills and schedule recurring payments
- Deposit checks using mobile deposit (up to $5,000/day)
- Set up account alerts and notifications
- Locate nearby ATMs and branches
- Contact customer support via in-app chat

4.2 Online Bill Pay
Set up bill payments through online banking:
- One-time or recurring payments
- Pay any company or individual in the US
- Electronic payments arrive in 1-2 business days
- Check payments arrive in 5-7 business days
- You can schedule payments up to 365 days in advance

Chapter 5: Security & Privacy
------------------------------

5.1 Fraud Prevention
SecureBank uses multiple layers of security:
- 256-bit SSL encryption for all online transactions
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) via SMS or authenticator app
- Real-time transaction monitoring and alerts
- Chip-enabled debit and credit cards
- Biometric login (fingerprint/face recognition) on mobile app

5.2 Privacy Policy
We are committed to protecting your personal information:
- We never share your information with third parties for marketing
- Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit
- You can request a copy of your data at any time
- Account data is retained for 7 years after account closure
- You can opt out of promotional communications at any time

5.3 Reporting Suspicious Activity
If you suspect fraud or phishing:
- Call 1-800-FRAUD-STOP (1-800-372-8378) immediately
- Forward suspicious emails to security@securebank.com
- Do NOT click on links in suspicious emails or texts
- Report the incident to the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov
